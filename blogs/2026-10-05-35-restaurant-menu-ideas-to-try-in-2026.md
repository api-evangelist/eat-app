Choosing what to put on a restaurant menu sounds creative until prep time, food cost and guest expectations all have to line up. A dish can look great online and still be a poor fit if it needs a new station, uses ingredients nowhere else on the menu or takes too long to plate during a Friday rush. The strongest restaurant menu ideas start with two questions: will people want it, and can your kitchen serve it consistently?
